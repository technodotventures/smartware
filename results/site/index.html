How well do agent memory systems recall?

The ORP reference engine against LLM-free re-implementations of the recall paths of 15 open-source memory projects, each given its best dev-tuned settings and the same encoders. Across 18 benchmark-metric comparisons with the best of the 15 on each metric, ORP is better on 7, level on 6 and worse on 5 (paired bootstrap, 95%).

What ORP specifies

Rank only what the asker may read

Access is decided before scoring, statistics come from readable memories only, and recall must equal recall over the store with everything unreadable removed.

Three clocks

Observed, happened and valid time on every record; every query carries an as-of time, so answers never drift with the wall clock.

Supersession you can audit

Newer claims replace older ones by validity time, only with write permission and never hiding a fact from someone who cannot see its replacement.

Context, not lists

Recall returns a token-budgeted, cited, explained context pack with coverage and degradation flags.

Derived never outranks its sources

Summaries and profiles inherit the narrowest audience and the sensitivity labels of what they were built from.

Testable

70 numbered requirements, each mapped to a conformance test, plus this benchmark with its raw per-question results.

Read these numbers with care

  • The 15 are re-implementations of each project's recall path without an LLM, written from its source code, not the products. Projects that rely on LLM extraction or reranking are represented by deterministic stand-ins.
  • Every system uses the same modest encoder (PPMI-SVD word vectors), trained without labels on the benchmark text. A strong neural embedder could change the order.
  • ORP had a larger tuning budget than any single baseline. The hand-tuned, model-free ORP variant is shown alongside for a like-for-like comparison of architecture.
  • LME-R is a reconstruction of LongMemEval, not the official benchmark. Numbers are not comparable to published LoCoMo or LongMemEval accuracies.
  • Test results from the pre-audit version had been seen before the current engine was built, so LoCoMo and LME-R are not pristine held-out sets for it. HotpotQA-50 is.
Specification: ORP 0.1 draft. Methodology, two independent audits and raw per-question results ship with the repository.
